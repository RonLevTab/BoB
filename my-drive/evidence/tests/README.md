## Cross-test raw material (input for the synthesis, not final text)

Facts pulled from the three results. The judgement calls in the synthesis below are
mine; this table is only what the tests actually say.

1. **All three agree on perseverance + drive to achieve.**
   BDC perseverance 16/16 (max) and need for achievement 19/20 · mba.com type is literally
   "Resilient Learner" (perseverance, resilience, rebuilds after pushback) · 16personalities
   names "high self-motivation", "results oriented", "decisive action". Strongest anchor available.

2. **Contradiction 1 — ambiguity.** 16personalities lists "discomfort with ambiguity" as a
   career weakness; BDC scores tolerance for ambiguity / stress at 23/24 (avg 19). These cannot
   both be true. Decide which matches the Pump & Go pilot week and say why.

3. **Contradiction 2 — control.** 16personalities lists "preference for control" and
   "reluctant to delegate"; BDC puts lure of power/control at 14/20, the *only* sub-score below
   average, while self-sufficiency/freedom sits above average (13/16). Possible reconciliation:
   wanting control over *my own* work and decisions is not the same as wanting authority over
   *people* or the status that comes with it. Worth testing against how the Boostcamp team ran.

4. **The soft spot both harder tests circle.** Working through other people:
   16personalities "reluctant to delegate" + "discomfort asking for support"; BDC low
   power/control (little pull toward organising and mobilising people and resources).
   This is the clearest weakness-to-SMART-goal candidate for the vision board.

5. **Quietest number.** Belief in ability to influence destiny = 20/24, exactly the cohort
   average, and the lowest-ranked thing in an otherwise high profile. Everything else says
   "acts hard"; this one says the conviction that acting changes the outcome is only ordinary.
   Either a measurement artefact or the most interesting sentence in the whole write-up.

## Synthesis (goes on the vision board, ~150 words)

1. **Where all three agree.** The trait that shows up in every result. This is a real
   strength, use it as the anchor of goal 1.
2. **Where they contradict each other.** One test says X, another says the opposite.
   Say which one matches what actually happened in Petaron and Pump & Go, and why.
3. **What I recognise from practice.** One concrete moment (Petaron sales call,
   Pump & Go pilot, pitch on 4 Sep) that proves the strength.
4. **What I do not recognise / reject.** At least one. Naming a result you disagree
   with, with a reason, is what makes this reflective instead of a horoscope.
5. **The weakness that becomes a goal.** The weakest point that turns into one of the
   3 SMART goals on the vision board.

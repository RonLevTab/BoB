# CLAUDE.md

Personal planning and evidence repo for Ron's BUAS minor "Building your own
Business" 2026-27 (30 ECTS). Goal: highest possible grade, with every piece of
work evidenced through GitHub issues on `RonLevTab/BoB`.

## Start of every session
1. `gh issue list -R RonLevTab/BoB --state open` and flag anything overdue or due within 7 days.
2. Check `priority:critical` issues first: those are fail-if-missed.
3. Ask what Ron did since last time and log evidence before planning new work.

## Issue conventions
- One milestone per issue (Boostcamp, Team up for Business, My Drive, My Dream).
- Exactly one `type:*` label, one or more `rubric:*` labels, matching `ilo:*`, and `phase:*` where it applies.
- Title: imperative, specific, with the date if it is a deadline ("Hand in Boostcamp pitch deck (Thu 10 Sep)").
- Body: what the rubric asks (quote the pass criterion), a checklist, and an **Evidence** section that must be filled before closing.
- Never close an issue without evidence linked (repo path, screenshot, photo, CSV, certificate, Brightspace confirmation).
- Group work: log Ron's own contribution; link to the shared team file.

## Repo layout
- `information/` course PDFs. Read these for rules, rubrics, and dates; do not edit.
- `boost/` Boostcamp (Pump & Go). `team-up/`, `my-drive/`, `my-dream/` per assessment.
- Large media (vlogs, videos) stays out of git; link to OneDrive/Brightspace instead and add a small still or thumbnail.

## Rules from the course
- AI level 5: high AI use allowed, but must follow the BUAS AI ethics policy and AIAS. Ron must be able to explain and defend every artefact. Keep a note of AI assistance where a deliverable asks for it.
- Rubric pass criteria are the definition of done. Quote them in issues.
- Deadline conflicts (for example 10 Sep 09:00 vs 12:00) are resolved by taking the earlier time.

## Writing style for deliverables
- English unless the coach or commissioner asks otherwise. Financial model is currently in Dutch.
- Concrete numbers from real data (pilot CSVs, interviews) beat generic claims.
